About Me

I am just someone who likes digital painting and mixed media. My work often explores the intersection of nature, human emotion, and abstract forms. With a background in traditional fine arts, I bring classical techniques into the digital realm to create unique, evocative pieces.

Every piece is a journey, an attempt to capture a fleeting feeling or a complex thought in a visual medium. I believe art should not just be seen, but felt.
